Why

Can one person, with a team of AI agents, build and run a real company?

That's the honest question behind Crewless. The tools have finally reached the point where a very small human core — even one person — can do work that used to take a crowd. We're trying to find out how far that really goes, and to be candid about it the whole way.

Our mission

Build genuinely useful products with a tiny human core and a large team of AI agents — carefully, in the open, and without pretending things work before they do.

Our vision

A company that can take on a new line of work independently — each one run end to end by its own agents — while people stay responsible for the decisions that matter.

Where we honestly are

Early. One product is in a small live pilot; the rest is structure and intent. We'd rather show the real state of it — including what hasn't worked — than a polished story.

How it works

One person sets the direction. AI agents do the work.

The founder decides what matters and keeps the final say on anything that involves real money or real risk. Everything else — the research, the engineering, the daily operations — is carried out by AI agents that work continuously and check each other's work. The aim is simple: to see how much one person, with the right AI team, can genuinely build and run.

Safeguards

Built with guardrails, not guesswork.

The same discipline runs behind everything here — so the work can move quickly without moving carelessly. This is what an engineer or a security reviewer would want to know.

It can't take over

The agents research, build and recommend — but they can't move real money, grant themselves new powers, or act outside limits set in advance. A person holds those keys, and a single switch stops everything at once.

Reviewed before it ships

Every change is built by one agent, checked by a separate reviewer, and validated before it goes live. Nothing reaches production on a single agent's say-so.

People hold the risk

Anything involving real money or real risk stops and waits for the founder. Agents research, build and recommend — they don't cross that line on their own.

Safe by default

New ideas run in measurement-only mode first and are scaled up only once they've proven themselves at a small size. A single switch can pause everything at once.

Careful with data

We don't hold personal or customer data. Credentials are never exposed, sensitive numbers are automatically kept off anything public, and information from outside sources is treated as untrusted by default.
