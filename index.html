How it works

One person sets the direction. AI agents do the work.

The founder decides what matters and keeps the final say on anything that involves real money or real risk. Everything else — the research, the engineering, the daily operations — is carried out by AI agents that work continuously and check each other's work. The aim is simple: to see how much one person, with the right AI team, can genuinely build and run.

01

Careful by design

Every new idea is tested thoroughly and measured against clear, honest results before it's ever acted on.

02

Checked, then checked again

Every piece of work is reviewed by another agent before it goes live, so quality is built into the process.

03

Safe and gradual

Nothing that carries real risk is scaled up until it has clearly proven itself on a small scale first.
