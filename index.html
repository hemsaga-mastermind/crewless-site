The brain

An AI that runs things — not one that just answers.

Most AI forgets the moment you close the chat — it can answer, but it can't be trusted to run something over days and months. Crewless is built the other way round: its agents carry the company with them and pick up exactly where they left off. That's the difference between a tool you prompt and a company that runs itself — and how we do it is the part we keep under the hood.

The moat

Keel

The reason our agents can be trusted to run things, not just answer — they don't forget, and they don't drift. Others reset every conversation; ours carry the company forward. How we do it is our edge.

The method

Proven or killed — before real money moves

No idea reaches real money on a hunch. Every one runs a gauntlet built to break it, and most don't survive. Only the few that do get armed — small, and only with a person's one-tap go. What's in that gauntlet stays under the hood.

The Lab

Ideas we're weighing — and the ones we've killed.

We work like an R&D lab: bring an idea, we build a proof-of-concept and find out honestly whether it has an edge. Not everything needs to be approved — we explore openly and drop what doesn't earn its place. A "no" is a finding, not a failure.

  • Exploring A self-healing watchdog for small websites — the tech we already run for ourselves.
  • Exploring An AI OS to quick-start a company — the Crewless apparatus, made portable.
  • Exploring Getting the most from AI, honestly — open-source-first, no lock-in.
  • Weighing AI that helps two people gauge genuine compatibility — privacy-first, anonymous, no personal data kept. A research question, not a product.
  • Killed Copying our approach onto other markets — tested, no real edge, dropped.
