The idea

Can one person, with a team of AI agents, build and run a real company?

One founder holds every decision on real money or real risk. A central team of AI agents grows, protects and monitors the company; each product runs on its own team of AI agents — its spine — and stands independently.

  • Reviewed before it ships
  • People hold the money & risk
  • It can't take over — one switch stops it
  • Proven small before it scales
  • No personal data held
  • Built in the open — setbacks included

The brain

An AI that runs things — not one that just answers.

Most AI forgets the moment you close the chat — useful for answers, not for running something over months. Crewless agents carry the company forward and pick up exactly where they left off. That's the gap between a tool you prompt and a company that runs itself — how we do it stays under the hood.

The moat

It doesn't forget

They don't forget, and they don't drift. Others reset every conversation; ours carry the company forward. How we do it is our edge.

The method

Proven or killed — before real money moves

No idea reaches real money on a hunch. It runs a gauntlet built to break it — most don't survive. Survivors get armed small, only with a person's one-tap go. What's in that gauntlet stays under the hood.

The shape

Independent products, one team each

Every product runs on its own team of AI agents — its spine — and stands on its own; the central team only watches over it. New products slot in without disturbing the ones already running. What actually exists today is named in What's real.
