We read the open job market so you don’t have to

Most job tools show you more listings. Listings are free and infinite. What actually costs you is the evenings spent applying to roles that were never going to say yes. So this is built to tell you which ones to skip, and why.

What you get back

  • A ranked shortlist of roles you can actually take — location and right-to-work checked, not just keyword-matched.
  • For each one: why it fits, the line in the posting that says so, and the reason they might turn you down.
  • The roles we think you should not apply for, with the disqualifier named.
  • If you send your LinkedIn export: which of these companies you already know someone at. A referral beats a better-scoring cold application.

Often the honest answer is “nothing this week”. We will say that rather than pad a list.

What we do with your CV

Plainly, because this is the part that matters and most services bury it.

  • You email it to us directly. There is no upload form on this page and no CV database. We did not build one, on purpose.
  • We use it for one thing: reading your skills and experience so we can match roles. Nothing else.
  • We delete it when the scan is done. The working copy lives only for the length of the run. We keep the shortlist we sent you, and nothing more.
  • We never sell it, share it, or send it to an employer. No recruiter gets your details from us. You apply yourself, on your own terms.
  • It is not used to train anything.
  • Ask us to delete everything and we will, same day, and confirm it.
  • Take out anything you would rather we never saw — photo, date of birth, address, ID numbers. We do not need any of it to match a job.

Ask for a scan

Right now a person runs each one and replies to you — so it is a handful at a time, not instant. Tell us three things:

  • Where you want to work. Countries or cities. This is your choice, not wherever you happen to live — searching India or the US from Sweden is normal.
  • What role you want next. It may not be the one you have now.
  • Anything that rules a job out — a notice period, no relocation, no night shifts, a visa you would need sponsored.

Email us your request

Attach your CV to that email. Sending it is how you give consent — the draft says so, and you can edit it before you send. Optionally attach your LinkedIn data export (Settings → Data Privacy → Get a copy of your data) and we will show you where you already know someone. No password, ever.
